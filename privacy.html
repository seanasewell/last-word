LASTWORD

Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Last Word: Word Game ("Last Word", "the app") is made by Studio Sewell, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

The short version. You can play as a guest without an email or phone number, or sign in with Apple or Google to keep your progress across phones. We store the name, optional photo and region you choose, plus your game results and progress, so we can run matches, leaderboards and rewards. We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell your data. You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Your account. If you play as a guest, the app creates an account identified by a random ID that isn't linked to your name, email or phone number. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive an account identifier and the email address from that service (with Apple you can choose to hide your real email). We use these only to sign you in and keep your progress. We don't email you and we don't show your email to anyone.
  • Your profile. The display name you choose, a profile photo if you add one, and the country and state or region you pick for the rankings. We don't use your device's location.
  • Game data. The words you play, your scores, placements and wins, your solo personal best, and a hidden difficulty level that adjusts how well the computer players play against you.
  • Progress. Your XP, level, achievements, daily and weekly challenges and play streak, along with your device's time zone so that "today" and streaks match your local day.
  • Safety data. Reports you make about other players and players you block, and reports other players make about your name or photo.
  • Technical data. Like any online service, our providers process your IP address and basic device and request information to deliver the service, keep it secure and prevent abuse (including an automated check that you're a person, not a bot, when you first sign up).

The app also saves a few settings on your device only, such as whether sound is on.

How we use it

  • To run the game: matchmaking, timing rounds, checking words, scoring and eliminations.
  • To show leaderboards, your stats, levels, achievements and challenges.
  • To sign you in and let you keep your progress on a new phone.
  • To keep the game fair and safe: preventing automated sign-ups and cheating, and acting on reports.
  • To fix problems and improve the game.

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it.

What other players can see

Your display name, profile photo, country, region and number of wins can appear to other players in matches and on the leaderboards. Choose a name and photo you're comfortable sharing publicly.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app. They process data on our behalf and only as needed to provide their service:

  • Supabase hosts our database, accounts and profile photos (servers in the United States).
  • Cloudflare Turnstile checks that new guest sign-ups come from a person.
  • Apple and Google sign you in if you choose Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google, under their own privacy policies.
  • Apple and Google also distribute the app through their app stores, under their own privacy policies.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of players or the integrity of the service.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, photo, sign-in details, results, progress, difficulty level and blocks are removed from our database straight away. Safety reports are kept so we can finish reviewing them, but they're no longer linked to your account. Copies in our providers' backups are removed on their normal backup schedule.

Your choices

  • Change or remove your name, photo and region at any time in the app's Profile tab.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account. This can't be undone.
  • Block any player by tapping them on the ranks or in a match's results.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to how it's used. Email us and we'll help.

Guest accounts are tied to the device they were created on. If you delete the app while playing as a guest, we can't restore that account, but you can ask us to delete its data by contacting us. If you've signed in with Apple or Google, sign in again on any phone to get your account back.

Children

Last Word isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected with access controls so that players can only change their own information. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.
