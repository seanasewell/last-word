Help and support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email sean@studiosewell.com. We usually reply within two business days.

How do I play?

You get one long word or phrase. Make as many smaller words as you can from its letters. Each letter can be used as many times as it appears in the source, and longer words score far more. In an elimination match, six players get the same word each round and the lowest score is out, until one player has the last word.

Which words count?

Real English words of two or more letters. Names, abbreviations and hyphenated words don't count, and neither does the source word itself (or any word of a source phrase). Enter one word at a time and press Enter to submit it.

Who am I playing against?

Real players and computer players. Computer players have a "Bot" badge next to their name. They play by the same rules and adjust to how you're doing.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Sign in with Apple or Google. On the welcome screen choose Sign in with Apple or Continue with Google, or if you're already playing as a guest, go to Profile → Save your progress. Your level, wins and achievements stay with you, and you can sign in on any phone to pick up where you left off.

I lost my account

If you signed in with Apple or Google, open the app and sign in with the same account. Guest accounts live only on the phone they were created on, so if you deleted the app or switched phones before signing in, we can't move that account over. You can start again by tapping Play as a guest.

Someone has an offensive name or photo

Tap them on the ranks or in a match's results and choose Report. We review every report and remove names and photos that break our rules. You can also block them, so they won't appear on your ranks or in your matches. Manage blocked players in Profile → Blocked players.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Delete account. This removes your profile, photo, wins, level and achievements right away. If you no longer have the app, email us and we'll delete your data.

I left a match. What happens?

Matches keep going without you, and you'll usually be eliminated at the end of the round. If you reopen the app while the match is still running, you'll go straight back in.
